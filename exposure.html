Exposure曝光

First-person photo puzzle level, single-player第一人称拍照解谜关卡，单人

A dark reactor hall with one rule: the eye sees the present, the camera shows what is real. Three code locks, a lift that has to be started from below, and no guide lines anywhere.

一座昏暗的核电站厂房，全关只有一条规则：肉眼看到的是现在，相机拍到的才是真的。三道密码门，一部要先从地下启用的电梯，全程没有一条引导线。

What I did我负责的部分

  • Drew the whole layout by hand and took it to a playable blockout in Unreal Engine 5.
  • Designed the camera rule and three code puzzles whose clues sit in the space, not in a menu.
  • Cut 268 lights to 54 and removed every floor line. Light, a glowing landmark and contrast carry the route.
  • 手绘整张布局，并在 Unreal Engine 5 里做成可玩的灰盒。
  • 设计了相机规则和三道密码谜题，线索都放在空间里，不放在界面上。
  • 把 268 盏灯减到 54 盏，删掉所有地面引导线，只靠灯光、发光的地标和空间对比带路。

Design goals设计目标

  1. One rule, taught by doing一条规则，靠动手教会

    The eye sees the present. The camera shows what is real. Three small rooms each teach one case of that rule before any lock asks for it.

    肉眼看到的是现在，相机拍到的才是真的。开头三个小房间各教这条规则的一种情况，之后才有锁来考它。

  2. Clues live in the space线索放在空间里

    No code is written where the eye can read it. One is on a note, one is a place to stand, one is spread over four rooms. Each lock asks for a better grasp of the space than the last.

    没有一个密码写在肉眼能读到的地方。一个在纸条上，一个藏在站位里，一个分散在四个房间。每一把锁，都比上一把更考验玩家对空间的把握。

  3. No guide lines不画引导线

    No floor lines, cables or arrows. The route is carried by a few strong lights, warm light at rest points only, and a glowing core tower that can be seen across the hall.

    没有地面线、线缆和箭头。带路的只有几盏强光、只在休息点出现的暖光，以及隔着大厅也能看到的发光堆芯塔。

Walk Exposure in eight beats分八步走完《曝光》

The line on the plan is the path of a scripted run in the packaged build: 234 seconds from spawn to the final white-out. Where a picture is split in two, the top half is the naked eye and the bottom half is the same view through the camera.

平面图上的路线，取自打包版里一次脚本通关的实际轨迹：从出生点到最后全白，234 秒。图片分成上下两半时，上半是肉眼所见，下半是同一位置举起相机后的画面。

Beat 1 of 8第 1 步，共 8 步

Three rooms, one idea each教学三间：一间只讲一件事

Pick up the camera and photograph a blank board: the words only exist in the viewfinder. The next room has a trench, and its bridge is only there through the camera. The third has a crate only the eye can see, so you walk straight through it. Every later puzzle is built from these three cases.

拾起相机，对着空白的告示板拍一张：字只出现在取景器里。第二间有一道地沟，桥板只有相机看得见。第三间有一只货箱，只有肉眼看得见，人可以直接穿过去。后面的每道谜题，都由这三种情况组合出来。

Beat 2 of 8第 2 步，共 8 步

The duty room: a code in a photo值班室：密码在照片里

The first locked door has a keypad on the wall beside it. The note next to it is scrambled to the eye and reads 472 in a photo. The keypad screen lists the clues already photographed, so nobody has to memorise a number.

第一扇锁着的门，密码面板挂在门旁的墙上。旁边的纸条肉眼看是乱码，拍下来是 472。输密码时，已经拍到的线索就列在面板旁边，玩家不用硬记数字。

Beat 3 of 8第 3 步，共 8 步

The pond: one spot where the dots line up池塘：只有一个位置能看到数字

Past the duty room the hall opens up: a pond, a crane, raised walkways, and the core tower glowing far off. Six pillars stand in the water. From one marked spot at the rail, the camera shows 48 green dots on them that close into 258. Step sideways and the digits fall apart. This time the clue is the space itself.

出了值班室，大厅一下子打开：池塘、吊车、高架走道，远处是发光的堆芯塔。水里立着六根柱子。站到栏杆边的黄色标记上举起相机，柱子上的 48 个绿点合拢成 258；横移一步，数字就散了。这一次，线索就是空间本身。

Beat 4 of 8第 4 步，共 8 步

The gate opens, the lift doesn’t大门开了，电梯没开

258 opens the main gate to the control hall. Behind it is a lift with its door shut and a red lamp. The prompt says the car is waiting at B1 and has to be started from below. The player now knows where they will come back up, and has a reason to go looking for a way down.

258 打开通往中央控制厅的大门。门后是一部电梯，门关着，亮着红灯，提示写着“电梯停在 B1，需从下层启用”。玩家知道了自己以后会从哪里上来，也有了去找路下楼的理由。

Beat 5 of 8第 5 步，共 8 步

The outer ring and the broken grate外圈高道与断裂的格栅

Back at the fork, the middle steps join a raised walkway that circles the hall. One span is missing. Through the viewfinder the grate is there, so the player crosses with the camera up, seeing only 43° of the world. It is the tutorial bridge again, with height added. The walkway ends at the service shaft, and the way down is a plain staircase with no trick to it.

回到岔路口，走中间的台阶并入环绕大厅的高架走道。走道中间断了一截。举起相机，格栅就在那里：玩家得一直举着相机走过去，眼前只剩 43° 的视野。这是教学间那座桥的重演，只是这次脚下有了高度。走道尽头是检修井，下去的是一段普通楼梯，不加任何机关。

Beat 6 of 8第 6 步，共 8 步

Under the pond: four frames, one code池塘底下：四个窗口，一个密码

The basement gallery looks into the pond from below. Four standing pads face four framed openings: three windows, and one seen by looking up from a lower room. Through the camera each gives one digit: red 7, blue 3, orange 4, purple 9. The vault door carries four lamps in the same colors, in code order, so the door itself tells the player how to arrange them: 7349. Inside is the access card for the core.

地下观察廊从水下看池塘。四块站位踏板对着四个带色框的窗口：三扇是窗，还有一个要在下层房间里仰头看。举起相机，每个窗口给一位数字：红 7、蓝 3、橙 4、紫 9。库房门上有四盏同色的灯，按密码顺序排列，所以怎么排，门上就能读出来：7349。门里是堆芯通行卡。

Beat 7 of 8第 7 步，共 8 步

The first ride up第一次坐电梯上行

The B1 lift hall is next to the vault. Step in and the car runs by itself after one second. There are no buttons inside. The doors open on the lift hall from beat 4, seen from the other side. From now on the lift runs both ways: the basement is a 6.5 m ride from the control hall instead of a walk round the ring.

B1 电梯厅就在库房旁边。走进轿厢，一秒后自动运行，里面没有按钮。门打开，是第 4 步见过的那个电梯厅，只是这次站在门里。此后电梯上下都能坐：从控制厅到地下只是 6.5 米的一趟电梯，不必再绕外圈。

Beat 8 of 8第 8 步，共 8 步

To the core走向堆芯

Across the switchgear room, a bridge and the waste-treatment room, the route seems to run out. The camera finds a button the eye can’t see, and a hidden door opens. The access card goes on the reader beside the last door. Then it is a short walk towards the fissure in the core tower, and the screen fades to white.

穿过配电间、廊桥和废液处理间，路看起来到头了。举起相机，能找到一个肉眼看不到的按钮，暗门随之打开。在终点门旁的读卡器上刷通行卡，然后朝堆芯塔的裂口走过去，画面渐渐变白，结束。

Paper first先画纸图

The map started as hand-drawn sheets and a molecule map. They are drawn the same way up as the plan: the start at the bottom right, the goal at the top right.

这张地图最早是我手绘的纸图和一张分子图。它们和平面图朝向一致：起点在右下，终点在右上。

What the paper already had纸图上已经有的

  • A teaching room at the start, for learning to take pictures.
  • A locked room with a password and a note, straight after it.
  • Walkways that loop round the middle of the map and meet again.
  • A key room off the main path, and a hidden door in front of the goal.
  • 开头的教学间，用来学拍照。
  • 紧接着一间带密码和纸条的房间。
  • 绕着地图中部转一圈、最后汇合的走道。
  • 主路之外的钥匙房，和终点前的暗门。

What came later后来才有的

The basement gallery under the pond and the lift that only starts from below came out of iteration. The basement sheet was drawn after the fact, so it is not shown here as a starting point.

池塘底下的地下观察廊，和只能从下面启用的电梯，是迭代中才加进来的。地下层的纸图是事后补画的，所以没有放在这里当作起点。

Iteration: what I cut and what I kept迭代：删掉什么，留下什么

Every build was checked against in-game screenshots and a scripted run of the packaged game, not against tool reports. Six changes mattered most.

每一版都用游戏内截图和打包版的脚本通关来验收，不只看工具报告。影响最大的是这六处。

  • Problem问题Rule对应原则Change改法
  • Cables, hazard stripes and floor arrows ran along the route and did the player’s thinking for them.线缆、黄黑条纹和地面箭头沿着路线铺了一路，替玩家把路想完了。 No guide lines不画引导线 All 393 cable segments, the stripes and the arrows are gone. Nothing on the floor points anywhere.393 段线缆、条纹和箭头全部删掉。地面上不再有任何指路的东西。
  • 268 lights left nothing dark, so no single light meant anything.全场 268 盏灯，没有暗处，也就没有哪盏灯有意义。 Every light has a job每盏灯都有用途 54 lights. Each one lights something the player uses. Only the five rest points are warm.减到 54 盏，每一盏都照着玩家要用的东西，只有五个休息点是暖光。
  • The lift ran both ways from the start, so the basement could be reached without the outer ring or the shaft.电梯一开始就上下都能坐，不走外圈和检修井也能到地下。 Earn the shortcut捷径要自己挣 The car starts at B1. The upper door stays shut behind a red lamp until the first ride up. After that it runs both ways.轿厢初始停在 B1。上层的门关着、亮红灯，直到第一次从下面坐上来；之后上下都能用。
  • The keypad at the main gate stood on the floor in a bright yellow cabinet.D 大门的密码面板，是立在地上的一只亮黄色柜子。 Part of the place属于这个地方 All three keypads hang on the wall by their door frames, each inside its own pool of light.三块面板都挂到门框旁的墙上，各有一盏灯罩住。
  • A fall sent the player back to the last rest point, up to 81 m away.掉下去会被送回上一个休息点，最远要重走 81 米。 Fair recovery公平的重来 Respawn at the nearest point already reached: a rest point, the start of the section, or one of eight silent checkpoints. In two test falls the player came back 7 m from where they left the floor.回到最近一个已经到过的点：休息点、这一段的起点，或八个不显示的检查点之一。两次实测跌落，重生点离掉下去的位置都是 7 米。
  • At the last door, the open leaf of the hidden door covered the card reader.终点门前，打开的暗门门扇正好挡住读卡器。 Visibility可见性 Reader moved 2.7 m onto the fixed door frame. It is fully in view when the prompt appears, checked in the packaged build.读卡器平移 2.7 米，装到固定门框上。提示出现时整个读卡器都在画面里，并在打包版中复查过。

One flag I overruled. A cleanup pass listed the four standing pads in the gallery as floor patches to delete. I kept them. They are the only hint for where to stand, and each shares its color with a lamp on the vault door.

有一处我没有照改。清理时，观察廊里的四块站位踏板被列为该删的“地面补丁”。我把它们留下了：它们是“该站在哪”的唯一提示，颜色也和库房门上的四盏灯一一对应。

How I worked工作方式

This was a solo project. I drew the layout by hand, set the rules above, and played each build to accept or reject it. I decided what to fix and what to keep. The 234-second run is a scripted player in the packaged build. It shows the level can be finished, not how a first-time player reads it.

这是我一个人的项目。布局由我手绘，上面的原则由我来定，每一版也由我进游戏验收，决定通过还是打回。改什么、留什么由我决定。234 秒的通关是打包版里的脚本玩家跑出来的，它只能说明这一关走得通，说明不了第一次玩的人会怎么读它。

What’s next接下来

  • Outside playtests. Two things to watch: whether the way back to the fork is clear after 258, and whether the core tower pulls players from across the hall.
  • Sound: shutter, keypad, lift, and a radiation counter that ticks faster near a clue.
  • A recorded walkthrough of the final build.
  • 请别人来试玩。重点看两处：输完 258 之后，回岔路口的路是否清楚；隔着大厅，堆芯塔能不能把人吸过去。
  • 声音：快门、密码面板、电梯，以及靠近线索时越跳越快的辐射计数器。
