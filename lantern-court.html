Lantern Court灯笼院

Interconnected shortcut level, single-player单人箱庭关卡，以捷径回环为核心

Two layers, three keys and three shortcuts. One-way doors, keyed locks and a kick-down ladder make every route bend back toward spawn.

上下两层、三把钥匙、三条捷径。单向门、带锁的门和一架可以踹下的梯子，让每条路线最后都绕回出生点。

What I did我负责的部分

  • Designed the layout, gating and three shortcuts on paper, then took them to a playable blockout in Unreal Engine 5.
  • Specified the mechanics: one-way doors, keyed locks, a kick-down ladder and wall-run boots.
  • Audited player guidance against 16 behavior rules and shipped six fixes.
  • 在纸上设计布局、门锁关系和三条捷径，再在 Unreal Engine 5 里做成可玩的灰盒。
  • 定下机关规则：单向门、带锁的门、可踹下的梯子、行壁靴。
  • 按 16 条玩家行为规律审查引导，改掉 6 处问题。

Design goals设计目标

  1. See a shortcut before you open it捷径先见后开

    Each shortcut is first met from the side that won't open. Players loop round and open it from the other side, so the level explains its own shape.

    每条捷径，玩家都先从打不开的一侧见到它，绕一圈后再从另一侧打开。关卡的结构由玩家自己走明白。

  2. Locks you can read at a glance门锁一眼能读懂

    A door tells you whether it pushes open, needs a detour, or needs one specific key. Each lock shares a color and a motif with its key.

    看到一扇门，就知道它是能推开、要绕路，还是要找某一把钥匙。每把锁和它的钥匙用同一种颜色和纹样。

  3. An ability that leads back to old ground新能力带玩家回到旧地方

    The wall boots arrive late. They carry the player across two voids and back to places first seen from the other side.

    行壁靴出现得比较晚。它带玩家越过两处虚空，回到之前只从另一侧见过的地方。

Walk Lantern Court in eight beats分八步走完《灯笼院》

Beat 1 of 8第 1 步，共 8 步

Spawn: three ways out, all shut出生点：三条路都走不通

A one-way door behind you, a red-locked door beside you, rubble to the north. The only way on is a 2.2 m drop you can't climb back up. Players take it last, and they remember three places they will come back to.

身后是单向门，旁边是挂着红锁的门，北边被碎石堵住。唯一的出路是一个 2.2 米的落差，跳下去就回不来。玩家会最后才走它，也会记住这三个以后要回来的地方。

Beat 2 of 8第 2 步，共 8 步

Down: a checkpoint, then the pistons下层：先给检查点，再给活塞

The lower layer opens with a checkpoint, then a corridor of pistons. At its far end sit two setups for later: a door barred from the other side, and a wall with a crack in it.

下层从一个检查点开始，接着是活塞走廊。走廊尽头埋了两个伏笔：一扇从另一侧闩住的门，和一面带裂缝的墙。

Beat 3 of 8第 3 步，共 8 步

Back up: the lock before the key回到上层：先见锁，再找钥匙

The return stairs end facing a gold-locked door. Players meet the lock first, so the key hunt has a purpose. A side door, unlocked, leads toward the key.

回程楼梯的尽头正对一扇挂金锁的门。玩家先看到锁，找钥匙才有目的。旁边一扇没上锁的门，通向钥匙的方向。

Beat 4 of 8第 4 步，共 8 步

The hidden key and the ladder藏起来的钥匙和梯子

The gold key sits across a 3.7 m gap over a 5.2 m fall, out of sight from below. A retracted ladder at the edge can be kicked down from above, so a missed jump costs a short climb, not a second run through the pistons.

金钥匙在一个 3.7 米宽的缺口对面，缺口下方是 5.2 米的落差，从下层看不到它。缺口边有一架收起的梯子，可以从上面踹下去。这样跳失败只需要爬一段梯子，不用再走一遍活塞走廊。

Beat 5 of 8第 5 步，共 8 步

First shortcut: back at spawn第一条捷径：回到出生点

Through the gold door, the north loop circles round to the courtyard. Push one-way door A from its barred side and the bar drops away. You are standing at spawn.

过了金锁门，沿北环绕到庭院。从闩着的一侧推开单向门 A，门闩落下，眼前就是出生点。

Beat 6 of 8第 6 步，共 8 步

Wall boots over the void行壁靴：越过虚空

A shaft ladder leads down to the wall boots. The wall-run crosses a void panel by panel, with a timed key press at each one, and ends at a switch. All it tells you: a wall cracks somewhere to the south.

顺竖井的梯子下去，拿到行壁靴。附墙路线一块板一块板地越过虚空，每块板都要在限时内按对一个键，尽头是一个按钮。按下后只有一句提示：南边传来墙体碎裂的声音。

Beat 7 of 8第 7 步，共 8 步

Second shortcut and the payoff第二条捷径，伏笔回收

The way out is one-way door B, the one that wouldn't open in beat 2. Beside it the cracked wall has collapsed, and light from the cellar behind it spills into the corridor. Inside is the red key.

出口是单向门 B，就是第 2 步里打不开的那扇。门旁的裂墙已经塌了，暗室里的灯光照到走廊上。里面放着红钥匙。

Beat 8 of 8第 8 步，共 8 步

Home stretch收尾

The red key opens the door beside spawn, which holds the bronze key for the courtyard gate. Behind the gate a last wall-run leads to the goal, marked by a lantern pole you have been able to see since the north loop.

红钥匙打开出生点旁的门，里面是庭院门的青铜钥匙。门后是最后一段附墙路线，通向终点。终点立着一根挂灯笼的旗杆，从北环起就能看到。

Iteration: a guidance audit迭代：一次引导审查

With the blockout playable, I audited it against 16 player-behavior rules: visibility, forward bias, caution at irreversible drops, attraction to light and others. Checking the saved map zone by zone for sightlines turned up six guidance problems. Each one was fixed and then checked again in the build.

灰盒能玩之后，我按 16 条玩家行为规律（可见性、向前性、慎落性、趋光性等）做了一次审查，逐区检查存盘地图里的视线，找出 6 处引导问题。每一处都改了，改完再到游戏里复查。

  • Problem问题Rule对应规律Change改法
  • One-way doors gave no feedback from the locked side.从锁住的一侧走近单向门，没有任何反馈。 Visibility可见性 The locked face is bare boards and shows a prompt. The other face carries door rings and a crossbar that drops away when pushed.锁住的一面只有门板，走近会弹提示；另一面有门环和门闩，推开时门闩落下。
  • Two keys and four doors were nearly the same color.两把钥匙几乎同色，四扇门也几乎同色。 Visibility可见性 Padlocks on the keyed doors. Lock and key share a color and a motif: gold cloud, red lozenge, bronze bamboo.带锁的门挂上挂锁，锁和钥匙同色、同纹样：金色云纹、朱红方胜、青铜竹节。
  • A missed jump at the key gap meant redoing the whole piston corridor.跳缺口失败，要把活塞走廊重走一遍。 Caution at drops慎落性 A retracted ladder at the gap, kicked down from above. A fall now costs one short climb.缺口边加一架收起的梯子，从上面踹下。掉下去只需要爬一段梯子。
  • The secret cellar was darker than the corridor outside it.暗室比外面的走廊还暗。 Attraction to light趋光性 Cellar light raised from 45 to 280 lm and moved to the opening, so the collapsed wall is the brightest spot nearby.暗室的灯从 45 流明调到 280 流明，挪到洞口内侧。墙塌之后，洞口是附近最亮的地方。
  • The goal could not be seen until its gate opened.终点在开门之前完全看不到。 Reward seeking趋利性 An 8.7 m pole with a lantern behind the goal, visible from the north loop and the courtyard.终点后立一根 8.7 米高、挂灯笼的旗杆，从北环和庭院都能看到。
  • Prompts were debug prints and vanished in packaged builds.提示用的是调试打印，打包之后不显示。 Build check打包验证 Moved to UI prompts and verified in a Shipping build.换成界面提示，并在 Shipping 包里验证。

One flag I overruled. The audit marked the gold key as invisible from below. I kept it hidden. Finding it is the reward for exploring that side path.

有一处我没有照改。审查指出金钥匙从下层看不到。我保留了这个设计：它是留给愿意探索那条岔路的玩家的奖励。

How I worked工作方式

This was a solo project. I designed the layout, the mechanics and the flow, and decided what to fix and what to keep. Every change was checked in play-in-editor or a Shipping build before it stayed.

这是我一个人的项目。布局、机关和流程由我设计，改什么、留什么也由我决定。每项改动都先在编辑器试玩或 Shipping 包里验证过，才保留下来。
